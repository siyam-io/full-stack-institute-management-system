## 📷 2. Geotagged Photo Upload Schedule

Search engines prioritize profiles that frequently post high-quality, real-world photos.

### Step 1: Capture Standards
- Take photos using high-resolution mobile cameras or DSLRs.
- Do NOT upload generic stock photos or heavily filtered screenshots.
- Focus on:
  - Students cooking in CIB kitchen labs.
  - Mentors (Dewan Ismail, etc.) lecturing or plate arranging.
  - ISO-HACCP safety protocols (e.g., students wearing hairnets, clean stainless steel counters, temperature thermometers).
  - Front door signage and street views to help students navigate physically.

### Step 2: Upload Rhythm
- **Upload Frequency:** Minimum **3 photos per week**.
- **Geotagging Rule:** Ensure mobile GPS location services are turned ON when capturing photos inside the lab. This embeds physical coordinate metadata (Exif geotags) directly into the image files, signaling authentic local activity to Google's ranking algorithms.

## 💬 3. Review Solicitation & Response Workflow

A high rating (4.8+ stars) and frequent conversational reviews containing keywords (e.g., "chef course in Dhaka", "best cooking school") boost local search rankings.

### Step 1: Review Request Template
Send this template via WhatsApp or SMS to students upon course completion or during major practical exams:

> *"Hi [Student Name]! We hope you are enjoying your hands-on chef training at CIB. Could you please take 1 minute to share your honest experience on our Google Business profile? Your review helps other aspiring chefs find the right path! Rate us here: **https://g.page/r/YOUR_REVIEW_LINK/review**"*

### Step 2: Response Templates (English & Bengali)
Always respond to all reviews within **24 hours** to show high engagement. Incorporate local SEO keywords naturally in the response.

#### 5-Star Reviews (English Response)
> *"Thank you so much for the feedback, [Name]! We are glad you enjoyed learning 120+ recipes in our Dhanmondi kitchen lab. Best of luck with your professional chef career!"*

#### 5-Star Reviews (Bengali Response)
> *"ধন্যবাদ, [Name]! সিআইবি (CIB) ধানমন্ডি ক্যাম্পাসে আমাদের মেন্টরদের অধীনে হাতে-কলমে শেফ কোর্স সম্পন্ন করার অভিজ্ঞতা শেয়ার করার জন্য আপনাকে অসংখ্য ধন্যবাদ। আপনার উজ্জ্বল ভবিষ্যৎ কামনা করি!"*
