# 📰 SOP 26: Press Outreach & PR Backlink Building

> [!NOTE]
> This Standard Operating Procedure guides CIB management and public relations agents on how to leverage institutional news (accreditations, TV features, culinary achievements) to gain media mentions and build high-authority backlinks.

---

## 🎯 1. Identifying Strong News Hooks

Journalists only cover stories that are timely, unique, or carry high civic relevance. CIB has three main evergreen press hooks:

### Hook 1: Technical Education & National Development
- **Angle:** How CIB is training the next generation of certified professional chefs to meet global labor demands, thereby boosting remittance earnings for Bangladesh.
- **Trigger Events:** New state approvals from the National Skills Development Authority (NSDA), or graduating a batch where 100% of students secure jobs.

### Hook 2: Food Safety & Culinary Industry Standards
- **Angle:** Introducing international ISO 22000 and HACCP food safety kitchen hygiene audits to local vocational culinary schools.
- **Trigger Events:** Audit renewal, SGS Switzerland site visits, or public masterclasses led by Chef Dewan Ismail.

### Hook 3: Media Broadcast Features
- **Angle:** Highlighting national television coverage (e.g., CIB's feature on Bangladesh Television - BTV).
- **Trigger Events:** Re-broadcasting, special television appearances, or major charity culinary events.

## 🔗 4. Converting PR Mentions into Backlinks

A news mention is good, but a clickable link to CIB is a goldmine for search engine rankings.

### Step 1: Monitor Brand Mentions
- Set up **Google Alerts** for keywords: `"Culinary Institute of Bangladesh"`, `"CIB Dhanmondi"`, `"Dewan Ismail chef"`.
- Audit new search results weekly to identify articles that mention CIB.

### Step 2: Link Reclamation Pitch
If a media outlet mentions CIB but does not include a link, email the editor or journalist with this pitch:

> *Dear [Journalist Name],*
>
> *Thank you for featuring the Culinary Institute of Bangladesh in your recent article, "[Article Title]"! We truly appreciate the coverage of our culinary academy.*
>
> *Would it be possible to add a hyperlink to our homepage (**https://cibdhk.com**) on the word "Culinary Institute of Bangladesh" in the [2nd/3rd] paragraph? This will help your readers easily navigate to our campus location, admission requirements, and course details.*
>
> *Thank you for your time and continued support of vocational education in Bangladesh.*
>
> *Best regards,*  
> *[Your Name]*
